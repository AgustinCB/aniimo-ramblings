I will cover in this guide which Aniimo to prioritize 
when building early and that will carry you through most
of the content. 

I will try to use the following criteria:

1. It is relevant in late game content. Early game
   content can be done with basically anything. 
2. It is accessible. If it is available early, in an egg
   through the season quests, or for free in an event, it
   gets priority.
3. It is flexible. Certain Aniimo are useful only on one
   type of content (Leafy mostly works for Egg Heist,
   for example). We want to prioritize Aniimo that work 
   in multiple modes.

There will be two categories of Aniimo:
Flexible/splashable and elemental cores.

Flexible or Splashable Aniimo are those that you can put
on any team and have them work fine. Elemental cores are
groups of Aniimo that work together because they share
an element and enhance each other in combat through that.

Usually, when team building, you will pick your elemental
core (which will have a DPS and maybe a Break) and then 
add the rest of the team from your pool of flexible 
Aniimo (usually supports).

When I speak about support Aniimo I am not referring only
to the Support class, but to the group of Regen, Heal, 
and Support classes all lumped together.

## Splashable Aniimo

These are _usually_ support. They are a great investment
because you will use them _all the time_. Because they
usually stay in combat for two seconds to cast a skill,
they are also cheap to build: You just need to put 
resources on the stat that scales that skill.

### Buffers

Two main splashable buffers: Turbo and Tromber.

![Turbo](/images/aniimo/turbo.png)

Turbo is probably the priority. If Prismana, it works
both as a splashable buffer and as part of the elemental
core for Dark. Its core skill buffs the damage of the
whole team and increases break efficiency. Every team is 
better when the buff is active. The increased break
efficiency also allows you to forget about bringing a
designated break: You can use your DPS with break shred
instead. Only focus on the ATK stat. It wants its 
enhanced skill, but there are alphas available so you 
don't necessarily need to contract.

![Tromber](/images/aniimo/tromber.png)

Tromber is a bit more specific, but equally useful: It 
gives healing over time, movement speed, and crits. This
is important because a lot of meta DPS and break Aniimo 
scale off crits (for example, Fulmintis). It is also 
probably the best support in certain modes like Egg
Heist. Although there is no alpha boss for it, there is
a Chirpi egg that you can evolve to Tromber. This is 
good: Tromber only works with its enhanced skill. Focus
only on the ATK stat.

### Healers

![Glacy](/images/aniimo/glacy.png)
![Gracewing](/images/aniimo/gracewing.png)

Two main ones: Glacy (Prismana if possible) and 
Gracewing. Glacy is better at burst heals and Gracewing
provides better passive constant healing off the bench.

Either one works. Glacy wants its enhanced core skill,
but Gracewing doesn't need it. There is an Alpha Glacy
to solve that problem without making a contract.

Prismana Glacy also acts as buffer (enhances your team
damage by 10%) and Gracewing can cleanse statuses and
elemental stacks.

In both cases, focus only on the HP stat.

![Glameep](/images/aniimo/glameep.png)

Glameep is supposed to be just as good, however: a) it 
is available late game and b) I haven't tested it.

However, it does provide utility that the other two 
don't: It has an anti heal skill to counter other heals.

### Regen

![Somniwing](/images/aniimo/somniwing.png)
![Besauce](/images/aniimo/besauce.png)

Two good options here, both free through main content:
Somniwing and Besauce.

They differ in similar ways as the healers: Somniwing 
provides EP over time and Besauce burst EP.

Both of them work without their enhanced core skill and
both of them want a focus on REGEN.

### Breaker

![Inferlupa](/images/aniimo/inferlupa.png)

Only one splashable break: Inferlupa. Its passive allows
it to shred the break shield of bosses even when doing
neutral element damage.

Because going through the break gauge does not account
for elemental advantages, Inferlupa is very 
splashable in almost any team. Even in cases where 
there is a disadvantage, you are only missing some
bulk and minimal DPS.

It can do without the enhanced skill, but there is an
alpha egg for Emberpup that can give you the enhanced
version for free. 

This one is not cheap, and you will likely want to build
multiple stats, in this order of priority: BREAK, HP,
defenses, ATK. This is a common trait of all breaks, 
as they stay in the field the longest and use most of 
their stats.

## Elemental cores

The first lesson you need to learn about elemental cores
is that you don't need to have a core for each element.

A lot of elements have either a poor offensive profile
(Earth) or their main DPS has better options in other 
elements (Ice). 

You can definitely ignore Earth and Ice. If you don't do
Egg Heist, you can also ignore Grass. And here comes The
Hot Take (TM): Ignore Light too. Your starter is very 
strong, however, there are no Light supports to enhance
its output like other DPS have. No Light break to have
synergy. And Wind DPS are just as good versus Dark 
without also being weak to it. By all means use Lunara
or Helion, but don't worry about spending too many 
resources on them.

With that, the cores you wanna worry about, in order of
priority.

### Dark

Arguably the best offensive type (very few relevant
resistances) with some of the best Aniimo in the game.

![Turbo](/images/aniimo/turbo.png)

For support, Prismana Turbo with its signature skill.
Doesn't need enhancement and can have investments only
on attack.

![Ignitis](/images/aniimo/ignitis.png)
![Stellarys](/images/aniimo/stellarys.png)

For DPS, one of Ignitis or Stellarys. Ignitis is better 
for crowds and Stellarys is better to kite individual
bosses. They both want their enhanced skills and both
have alpha available. Ignitis can use crit items and
Stellarys fission needle.

For stats, Stellarys usually works to burst down broken
enemies, so it can just focus on ATK. Ignitis often 
wants to stay longer, finishing grouped enemies, so 
it can use some love on HP and the defenses.

If you need a break, Inferlupa is your guy.

![Ekrim](/images/aniimo/ekrim.png)

Ekrim works really well as mixed DPS/Break and it is 
very fun, but also late game.

### Lightning

Lightning is a great type for two reasons: Fulmintis is
the best DPS in the game and the type provides perfect
coverage when combined with Dark: No type combination
resists both.

![Luminelle](/images/aniimo/luminelle.png)

For support, you can use either your splashable Turbo
(Fulmintis benefits from crits) or the more specific
Luminelle. Both want investment only on ATK. Both want
their enhanced skills. Luminelle has an alpha to get it
for free.

![Fulmintis](/images/aniimo/fulmintis.png)

For DPS, as already mentioned, Fulmintis. Build it fully
prioritizing ATK, HP, and defenses. Its enhanced skill 
is better, but it works fine without it. Use crit items.

![Blazen](/images/aniimo/blazen.png)

Blazen is a fine Break, but nothing to write home about.
Inferlupa is not useful versus the types Fulmintis is 
good against (they resist Dark). But that is not a 
problem: With Luminelle, you don't need a break. It 
gives Fulmintis increased break efficiency and allows 
her to play as a mixed Break/DPS. 

### Wind

Wind is useful because a lot of content features Dark 
mobs. It also has very strong Aniimo with good stats
and performance. 

![Tromber](/images/aniimo/tromber.png)
![Mesmerowl](/images/aniimo/mesmerowl.png)

For support, your splashable Tromber does the trick. 
Mesmerowl is also good, but very late game.

![Hypnyto](/images/aniimo/hypnyto.png)

For DPS, Hypnyto is available for free through an egg
in the season progress rewards, and one of the 
strongest DPS in the game. She wants the enhanced skill
but there is no alpha, so you will need to make a 
contract. Like other DPS, fully prioritize all relevant
stats: ATK, HP, and defenses in that order. Use crit 
items.

![Helgon](/images/aniimo/helgon.png)

For break, Helgon is one of the best breaks in the game.
It is also very fun to use: Its strength comes from a 
parry mechanic that enhances its break efficiency. 

It wants the enhanced skill, but there is an alpha 
available. Like all breaks, it is expensive, it wants 
(in order): Break, HP, ATK, and defenses.

That said, if you use Turbo, Hypnyto does so much DoT
that she works well as a mixed BREAK/DPS.

### Fire

The Fire element is nice because all of its meta options
are available early and they shower you with resources
for them. 

There are also a lot of Grass mobs in the initial main
quest progress. 

![Sparkelf](/images/aniimo/sparkelf.png)

For support, Sparkelf is free from an event and 
objectively the best. There is no alpha and its enhanced
skill is really nice, but you do get a lot of value just
from the base one, so don't sweat too much if you don't
have resources for a contract. It only needs investment
on its ATK.

![Scorchhowl](/images/aniimo/scorchhowl.png)

For DPS, the answer is Scorchhowl. Great kit with a lot
of burst damage and mobility, accessible right away, and
with synergy with multiple supports and breaks in the 
game. It _needs_ the enhanced skill, so either use the 
alpha or make a contract. The good part is you often 
want to use him on broken or stunned bosses to do a 
bunch of burst damage before going back to the bench. So
you can ignore all stats but ATK and just max that. Use
the explosive globes as an item.

![Squashel](/images/aniimo/squashel.png)

For break, use Inferlupa when fighting Grass and 
Squashel when fighting Ice. Squashel wants its enhanced
skill (and has no alpha available) and uses the common,
expensive build for her class: Break, HP, ATK, and 
defenses in that order of priority.

### Water

The Water element is really useful, but a bit
late-gamey. A lot of Fire bosses make the element really
important, though, so it is not a bad investment.

![Dewple](/images/aniimo/dewple.png)

For support, there are not a lot of great early game
supports for Water. Erlath and Piopiota are fine, but
you get more bang for your buck just using Turbo
instead. Or even Panpanta as a pseudo-support. Late 
game, Dewple is fantastic.

![Sherro](/images/aniimo/sherro.png)

For DPS, Sherro. It is also kinda late-gamey, but it is
really effective and has fantastic burst. You can use it
as an ult bot ignoring all other skills, so it doesn't 
necessarily need the enhanced skill. There is an alpha 
egg, though, so it is not expensive to get the enhanced 
version. Because you use it for burst on stunned or 
broken bosses, you can just focus on ATK. Use the 
capacitous battery and focus on spamming ults from 
puddles on the ground.

![Panpanta](/images/aniimo/panpanta.png)

For break, Panpanta. It is available early, has great
stats, and serves as pseudo-support for Sherro: It 
breaks shields by placing puddles on the ground that
the DPS can later use to do more damage. Same build
priority as other breaks.

### Grass

Grass is not a bad type, but it suffers from having to
compete with Lightning and Water cores that are usually 
more reliable. If fighting Earth, you would rather 
use Water. If fighting Water, you would rather use 
Lightning. So in most cases, you can just ignore Grass.

However, their main core has one important quality: It
is arguably the best core in the Egg Heist mode. So if 
you are engaging with that mode a lot, it is worth 
using. 

![Melloblum](/images/aniimo/melloblum.png)
![Leafy](/images/aniimo/leafy.png)

For support, you want Melloblum and Leafy. Leafy wants
the enhanced core skill, but Melloblum doesn't care.
Leafy wants to focus on REGEN and Melloblum on ATK. 

![Irisalis](/images/aniimo/irisalis.png)

For DPS, you want Irisalis. She is fantastic in every
way and cheap: You get an item that has a pact with her
already formed and she is granted through a free quest.

Throw Turbo in to turn Irisalis into a mixed BREAK/DPS
and you have a full team that shreds through bosses and
Egg Heists.

## What about items

Don't worry too much about over-optimizing for them. For
the DPS, I listed the items they normally want. But all
of them work just as well with ferocious fang. 

For breaks, gargantuan horn works fine on all of them.

For healers that rely on HP, marching flask or the 
miraculous fleece.

For regens, spirited feather. For supports, ferocious
fang.
